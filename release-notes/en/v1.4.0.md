# TExec v1.4.0

## Appearance themes

- Choose from ten palettes: TExec, Ocean, Indigo, Nord, Dracula, Solarized, Gruvbox, Catppuccin, Rosé Pine and Graphite.
- Three additional options provide higher contrast: High contrast, Amber contrast and Blue & orange. They strengthen borders and keyboard focus; Blue & orange also distinguishes success and error without a red/green pair.
- Every palette includes light and dark variants. The existing sun/moon switch remains independent of palette selection.
- Administrators can choose the global default under **Management → General settings → Appearance**. It applies to the login page and accounts without a personal override.
- Users can choose a personal palette under **Account → Appearance**, immediately below **Edit account**. **Default** inherits the global choice; the global **Default** is the original TExec palette.
- Palette cards preview the appearance before saving. Global and personal preferences are stored in the database; a personal preference follows the account across browsers.
- Palettes cover login, navigation, controls, charts and workflows. The TExec logo, wordmark and browser favicon now follow the active palette and light/dark mode.

## Upgrading

No database schema migration or new deployment settings are required for appearance preferences. The original TExec palette remains the default. Light/dark mode remains a separate browser preference.

Container images are published to Docker Hub and GHCR for `linux/amd64` and `linux/arm64`. Helm chart **1.4.0** is published separately.
